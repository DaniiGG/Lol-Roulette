**Approach**: Each article targets a specific search intent, links naturally to related content, and includes a contextual CTA to the app. No filler, no keyword stuffing.

## Content Production Checklist per Article

- [ ] Keyword research (Ahrefs/SEMrush or free tools)
- [ ] Search intent verified (SERP analysis)
- [ ] Outline: H1, 4-6 H2s, 1 table, 1 FAQ (if genuine)
- [ ] Draft: 800-1,500 words, scannable, bullet-heavy
- [ ] Internal links: 3-5 (same cat, cross cat, LR, app)
- [ ] Images: hero + 1-2 inline (custom or CC0)
- [ ] Schema: Article + Breadcrumb + FAQ (if applicable)
- [ ] EN publish → ES translate → QA → publish other locales
- [ ] Add to sitemap, submit GSC
- [ ] Monitor: impressions, clicks, CTR, scroll depth at 2 weeks

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Duplicate content across locales | `hreflang` only for translated locales; canonical to EN |
| Thin category pages | Noindex until ≥3 articles; show "Coming soon" |
| Patch content decay | Auto-archive patch posts > 6 months; link to live /tierlist |
| Keyword cannibalization | Quarterly audit: merge or redirect overlapping posts |
| Translation quality | Professional for P0/P1; MTPE + native review for rest |
